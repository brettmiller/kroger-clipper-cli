# kroger-clipper

Attaches Kroger digital coupons to a shopper's loyalty account automatically, so
the savings are already in place before a store visit rather than being clipped
by hand at the shelf.

## Language

**Digital Coupon**:
A discount a shopper attaches to their Loyalty Account ahead of time, which
applies automatically at checkout when a qualifying item is purchased.
_Avoid_: Coupon, offer, deal, savings

**Cash-Back Offer**:
A saving that pays into a rewards balance after purchase rather than discounting
at the register. Out of scope, and not a kind of Digital Coupon.
_Avoid_: Coupon, rebate

**Clip**:
To attach a Digital Coupon to a Loyalty Account. Clipping is not redeeming — a
clipped coupon applies only when a qualifying item is actually bought.
_Avoid_: Activate, add, load, redeem

**Loyalty Account**:
The Kroger shopper identity that Digital Coupons attach to.
_Avoid_: Account, user, profile

**Shopper Card**:
The card number identifying a Loyalty Account at the register.
_Avoid_: Loyalty card, rewards card, alt ID

**Session**:
Proven authentication as the holder of a Loyalty Account, established by a human
signing in and expiring on its own schedule.
_Avoid_: Login, credentials, token

**Banner**:
A grocery chain owned by The Kroger Co. that shares the same shopper platform —
Kroger, Ralphs, Fry's, Fred Meyer, and others.
_Avoid_: Chain, brand, store

**Store**:
A single location of a Banner. Which Digital Coupons exist depends on the Store
a Loyalty Account is set to.
_Avoid_: Location, facility
